## Erratum 2026-10-08 (#167): the staging ceiling named "default"

The "Pinned traffic" line above names `stage_cold` at 31.5 GB/s as the staging kernel. That is not the default: the default since #19e (2026-09-12) is `stage_cold_ca`, whose device-issued ceiling on this machine is 51.6 GB/s (copy engine 54.6 GB/s, `docs/architecture.md:1414`); `stage_cold` is the `CROW_STAGE_KERNEL=1` fallback. - Floor: 31.5 + 354.8 = **386.3 ms (2.6 tok/s)** at R = 51.6 GB/s, against 406.5 ms (2.5 tok/s) printed for 31.5 GB/s. At the copy engine's 54.6 GB/s: 29.8 + 354.8 = 384.6 ms (2.6 tok/s).
